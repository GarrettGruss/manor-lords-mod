# Identity blocks (task 1.2)

Fixed descriptions of every recurring person and object. Paste the block into each prompt that shows it. Change a block only with the owner's approval, and then remake every still that uses it.

## The narrator (lieutenant lord)

An original figure, not based on any real or fictional person. A tired Franconian lord of about forty. Short dark beard with some grey, dark hair to the neck, a lined, honest face. A plain dark-green wool coat over a mail shirt, a brown leather belt, dark hose, worn boots. No crown, no heraldry, no jewelry. Signature object: a plain sheathed sword with a dark leather grip. Allowed changes: posture, expression, the sword in hand or on the table. Reference still: `design/trailer/invasion-mod/storyboard/S10.jpg`.

## The burned house

The inside of a burned peasant house at night. Charred roof beams, part of the roof open to a dark sky, one wall broken through, soot on the walls, a small hearth fire on the right as the only light. A rough wooden table with a candle stub and a clay cup. Allowed changes: camera position, how much light the hearth gives. Reference still: `S10.jpg`.

## Jan Hus

A priest of about forty-five, bearded, in a plain dark robe, bound to a wooden stake. He is shown as in the Jena Codex tradition, in a paper heretic's cap. Allowed changes: none between `02-torch` and `03-stake`. Source: the Jena Codex page `design/trailer/references/wikipedia/Jan_Hus_at_the_Stake.jpg` (public domain).

## The Hussites

Commoners, men and some women. Padded coats, hooded cloaks in brown, grey and undyed wool, kettle hats, a little mail, no full plate. Flails, war hammers, spears, crossbows, tall painted pavise shields. No crosses or emblems on their clothes. A captain wears a kettle hat and a padded coat. Allowed changes: number and pose.

## The Hussite banner

A tall, narrow cloth banner hanging from a crossbar on a long pole, with a swallowtail end. A gold chalice (cup, stem, one round knob, wide foot; no gems) on a red field. No text. Reference: `openspec/changes/add-invasion-trailer/references/banners.png`, left panel.

## The king's seal

A round red wax seal with a single-headed black eagle, wings spread, on a gold field. Reference: `banners.png`, right panel.

## The war wagons

Heavy wooden farm wagons with high plank sides, chained wheel to wheel in a ring. No guns on them. Shown only in Manuscript panels, never in gameplay.
