MANUSCRIPT MOTION SYSTEM: the panel stays a painted page. Motion is slow parallax between ink layers, flickering fire light, drifting embers and smoke, and small subject actions. Faces, clothes, banners, props and the parchment texture stay stable. Camera grammar: locked, slow push-in, slow tilt, or a slow crane. Transitions burn through the page edge or follow fire and smoke. No photographic realism, no 3D camera orbit, no abrupt lens change, no temporal warping, no unrequested cut, no text, no logo, no watermark.
