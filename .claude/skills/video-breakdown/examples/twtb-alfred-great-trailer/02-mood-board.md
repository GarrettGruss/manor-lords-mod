# 02 Mood board

Frames are in `storyboard/S<nn>.jpg`. See `storyboard.jpg` for all of them on one sheet.

## Three looks

| Look | Used for | Light and color | Example frames |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Firelight** | The hero alone, thinking and deciding | Near-black background, one warm key light from a fire or candle, red cloak as the only saturated color | S06, S10, S14, S23, S28, S33, S34 |
| **Ash** | Victims, ruined villages, the enemy raids | Desaturated, almost black and white; fog; snow; grey sky; smoke columns | S07–S09, S11–S13, S16–S22 |
| **Daylight** | Peace, the muster, the battle | Natural, soft overcast; green grass; colored shields (red, yellow, blue) | S24, S25, S29–S32, S36–S52 |

The hero gets a clean daylight close-up only once (S35), at the moment he leads the charge.

## Camera language

- Firelight: locked or very slow push-in, shallow depth of field, faces half in shadow.
- Ash: slow tracking shots, people seen from behind or with heads bowed, nobody looks at the camera.
- Raid: hand-held, low angle, fast whip pans, shields fill the frame.
- Battle: hand-held inside the shield wall; a few high top-down shots (S40) for scale.
- End: a single crane up from ground level to the map (S53–S54).

## Recurring motifs

- The table with the candle and the cup (S10, S23, S28, S33–S34). The props change little; the light and his posture change.
- Shields as the face of each side: the enemy's black-and-white rune shields, the hero's red and yellow shields, later blue shields in the battle.
- Kneeling and bowed people (S09, S11, S13) against the standing, marching army later (S29, S32).
