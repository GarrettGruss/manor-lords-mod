## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-13

First pre-release for play testers.

### Added
- Each game month there is a 15 percent chance of a refugee wave. The village needs at least 10 families.
- A wave settles 1 to 3 groups of families near the woods (or near the village). The game puts up a tent camp. The mod never houses the refugees.
- A banner and a log line announce the wave.
- The wave ends when the homeless families have homes again.
- The refugees lose patience after 30 game days without a home, or when approval falls under 40. Then half of the homeless wave families leave and come back as a bandit raid.
- The mod needs no harness. It loads with the shared module ManorCore only.
