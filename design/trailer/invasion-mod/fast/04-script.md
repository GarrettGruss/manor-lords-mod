## Voice notes

- About 45 English words and three Czech lines in 56 s. The Alfred trailer has about 60 words in 75 s; the density is similar.
- The narrator speaks only in acts 1 and 2. He is silent from 0:44.5 to 0:55.5, during the turn, the fight and the rout.
- His lines get shorter: two lines of 5–6 words, then the long order, then 4–6 words, then 6 words for the choice, then nothing.
- The whisper at 0:55.5 is the only line after the fight. Who says it is an open point (see `README.md`).
