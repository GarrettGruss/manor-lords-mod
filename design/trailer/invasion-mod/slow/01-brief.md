# 01 Brief (slow version)

Status: draft (2026-10-22). Written by an agent at the user's request, from [`design/mod/trailer.md`](../../../mod/trailer.md) (the story, owned by the user) and [`design/mod/invasion-mod.md`](../../../mod/invasion-mod.md) (the mod design). The structure and pacing are inspired by the Thrones of Britannia "Alfred the Great" trailer (breakdown: `.claude/skills/video-breakdown/examples/twtb-alfred-great-trailer/`). No footage, frames or narration from that trailer are used.

## What we take from the Alfred trailer

1. (Not taken: the cold open. The opening is a different idea, from a second reference the user chose: a voice singing in the dark, then the fire as the first light. See `03-beat-sheet.md`, beats 1–2.)
2. One narrator, one person. Game systems appear as steps in his story, never as explanations.
3. A recurring anchor shot: the narrator at a table in a burned house, in ember light. It fits the mod: the liege gave him a burned village.
4. Escalation in short lines, each one proven by the next shots.
5. A still pause before the turn.
6. The victory shown through the enemy: the raiders look around, break and flee (the game's own rout, pillar 5).
7. A pull-back to the map at the end. Here it turns the victory back into dread (pillar 4): the great raid is still coming.

## What we do differently

- 60 s, not 110 s. The Alfred story part is 75 s; ours is 56 s. Every beat is shorter.
- The history is told in storybook panels, not in footage.
- The narrator hates the enemy. The history act shows why the Hussites came; the Franconia act shows what they do to his people. Both are true, and the trailer does not resolve them.
- The end is not a win. The battle is one raid; the map shows the great raid that is still coming.
